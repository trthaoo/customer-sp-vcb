# TikTok Funnel Specification (US Market)

## Purpose
Defines distinct operational workflows for the TikTok channel: short-form video comment interactions and TikTok Direct Messages.

## Funnel Characteristics
- **Scope**: TikTok platform only (platform: tiktok).
- **Public Video Comments**:
  - Keep replies under 1–2 sentences: vibrant, friendly, relatable, and social-native.
  - Boost engagement with the TikTok algorithm without cluttering comments with complex policy text.
  - Guide interested commenters to tap our profile avatar for the bio link or send a direct message.
- **Direct Message (DM) Consultations**:
  - Address specific questions about US ring sizing, sterling silver craftsmanship, delivery estimates, and checkout assistance.
  - Maintain a warm, welcoming, and relaxed tone.
