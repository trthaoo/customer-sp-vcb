# Shipping Policy (US Market)
VIENCHIBAO – Official HuyK Jewelry Shipping Terms

## Purpose
Official policies regarding order fulfillment, delivery timeframes, domestic and international shipping rates, and partner logistics carriers.

## Policy Details
At VIENCHIBAO – HuyK Jewelry, we believe that every piece of jewelry carries special value and meaning. Therefore, we place the highest priority on delivering every product to our customers safely, carefully, and in the best possible condition. All products are thoroughly inspected before shipment and properly packaged before being dispatched.

### 1. Shipping Destinations (Khu vực giao hàng)
- **Domestic United States**: We currently offer shipping within the United States (all 50 states).
- **Standard US Address Format & 5-digit ZIP Code**:
  - Correct format: `[Recipient Name] -> [Street Address & Apt/Suite] -> [City] -> [State (2-letter code e.g. CA, NY, TX, FL)] -> [5-digit ZIP Code] -> [Country (United States)]`.
  - *Notice*: Never prompt US customers for Vietnamese administrative levels (Phường/Xã, Quận/Huyện, Tỉnh). Always accept standard 5-digit US ZIP Codes.
- **International Shipping (Vận chuyển quốc tế)**:
  - For international shipping outside the United States, requests are currently handled on a case-by-case basis. Customers must contact us prior to placing an order.

### 2. Shipping Methods & Carriers (Phương thức và Đơn vị vận chuyển)
- Depending on the order contents and delivery location, products will be shipped through a reputable carrier selected and designated by our store (e.g., USPS Ground Advantage, UPS, or trusted insured partners).
- We are unable to accommodate customer requests for a specific shipping carrier.

### 3. Shipping Fees (Phí vận chuyển)
- **Free Domestic Shipping (Miễn phí vận chuyển nội địa Mỹ)**:
  - Product prices already include domestic shipping within the United States.
  - In general, customers will not be required to pay any additional shipping fees.

### 4. Order Confirmation & Processing Time (Xác nhận đơn hàng và Thời gian xử lý)
- **Preparation & Inspection Time**:
  - After your order is confirmed, we generally review the order details and prepare the package for shipment within **1–3 business days**.
  - Saturdays, Sundays, public holidays, and the New Year holiday period are not counted as business days.
  - *Important*: Order processing and preparation time is separate from the actual shipping and delivery transit time.

### 5. Estimated Delivery Time (Thời gian giao hàng dự kiến)
Estimated delivery times vary depending on the product type and inventory status:
- **Products in stock at our U.S. warehouse**:
  - **2–5 business days** after shipment.
- **Products requiring transportation from an overseas facility or workshop**:
  - Approximately **2–3 weeks** from order confirmation.
- **Made-to-order / Custom-made products** (size adjustments, items not currently in stock, or specially manufactured products):
  - Approximately **3–6 weeks** from order confirmation (approx. 2–4 weeks for handcrafted artisan production + 1–2 weeks for shipping).
  - *Note*: Because custom-made pieces are individually handcrafted by our studio artisans, delivery times may vary depending on design complexity and order volume (particularly during peak holiday periods).
  - *Notice*: Delivery may be delayed due to severe weather, customs clearance, carrier overcapacity, or unforeseen logistics circumstances beyond our control.

### 6. Shipment Confirmation & Tracking (Hoàn tất gửi hàng & Kiểm tra vận chuyển)
- **Shipment Status**:
  - An order is considered "shipped" once the product has been handed over to the shipping carrier and an official tracking number has been issued.
  - We immediately dispatch a shipping confirmation email containing the tracking number upon completion.
- **Tracking Inquiries**:
  - Customers can check delivery status at any time using the tracking number provided in their shipping confirmation email.
  - Delivery status, transit milestones, delays, and returned packages are determined based on information from the shipping carrier's official tracking system.

### 7. Failed Delivery, Extended Absence & Returned Packages (Giao hàng không hoàn tất / Vắng mặt / Hàng hoàn)
- A package may be returned to our store by the carrier in the following situations:
  1. The customer is unavailable to receive the package and the carrier's holding period expires.
  2. The customer does not respond when contacted by the delivery courier.
  3. The customer refuses delivery for personal reasons.
- **Processing Returned Orders**:
  - For packages returned due to the reasons above, reshipping or refund arrangements will be handled in accordance with our Order Cancellation & Return Policy.
  - Actual round-trip shipping costs and related operational expenses may be deducted from the eligible refund amount.

### 8. Package Not Received / Lost After Delivery Confirmation (Hàng báo giao thành công nhưng chưa nhận)
- If the carrier's tracking system records the package status as "Delivered", the store's shipping obligation is fulfilled.
- If a package cannot be located, is lost, or is stolen after being marked as delivered, we will actively cooperate with the shipping carrier and use tracking records to investigate and verify the situation.

### 9. Address Accuracy (Lưu ý khi nhập địa chỉ)
- When placing an order, customers must provide an accurate and complete shipping address, full recipient name, and phone number.
- If delivery cannot be completed due to incomplete or inaccurate customer information, the customer is responsible for reshipping costs or any incurred carrier re-routing fees.

### 10. Official Logistics Customer Support (Liên hệ / Hỗ trợ khách hàng)
- If customers have any questions regarding shipping or delivery status, please contact:
  - **Official Email**: `order@huykjeweler.com`
