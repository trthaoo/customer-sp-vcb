# Cancellation, Return & Refund Policy (US Market)
VIENCHIBAO – Official HuyK Jewelry Order Cancellation, Return & Refund Policy

## Purpose
Official studio guidelines governing order cancellations, eligible returns, size exchanges, refunds, and undelivered package processing.

## Policy Details
At our store, we put the dedication, craftsmanship, and hard work of our artisans into every piece we create. We take great care to provide you with jewelry of the highest quality. In the rare event that a product arrives with an initial defect or damage caused by our side, we will promptly and sincerely resolve the issue by accepting a return, exchange, or refund in accordance with this policy.

### 1. Order Cancellation (Hủy đơn hàng)
- **Cancellation Before Shipment (Trước khi gửi hàng)**:
  - Customers may cancel their order before the product has been shipped.
  - Even after payment has been completed, if the item has not yet been shipped, we will issue a full 100% refund without deducting any processing fees.
  - Refunds are processed through the original payment method (Credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, etc.) and may take 3–10 business days to appear on your bank statement.
- **Cancellation After Shipment (Sau khi đã gửi hàng)**:
  - We do **not** accept order cancellations once the order has been shipped or a carrier tracking number has been issued.
  - Please monitor delivery status using the tracking number provided in your shipping confirmation email.

### 2. Conditions for Returns, Exchanges & Refunds (Điều kiện đổi trả & hoàn tiền)
- **Eligible Cases - Store Responsibility (Lỗi từ phía cửa hàng)**:
  - Returns, exchanges, or full refunds are accepted in the following cases:
    1. The product arrives with an initial defect or damage (scratches, deformation, breakage, missing components, loose stones).
    2. You receive a product that is different from the item you ordered (wrong item delivered).
  - **Notification Window**: Customers must contact us **within 7 days of receiving the product**.
  - **Shipping Costs**: Vien Chi Bao covers **100% of all return shipping and reshipping costs**.
- **Ineligible Cases - Customer Responsibility (Lý do từ phía khách hàng - KHÔNG chấp nhận)**:
  - Returns, exchanges, refunds, or cancellations are **strictly NOT accepted** for customer-related reasons, including:
    1. The product does not meet personal expectations or the customer selected the wrong ring/bracelet size.
    2. The product has been used, even once.
    3. You contact us **more than 8 days** after receiving the product.
    4. The product has been damaged, bent, or broken due to improper use, mishandling, or accidents by the customer.

### 3. Undelivered Orders, Refusal of Delivery & Prolonged Absence (Hàng hoàn do từ chối nhận hoặc vắng mặt)
- **Failed Delivery Due to Customer Circumstances**:
  - The following situations are classified as customer-caused delivery failure:
    1. Customer is absent and the carrier's storage period expires.
    2. Customer does not respond to delivery carrier contact attempts.
    3. Customer refuses to accept the package for personal reasons.
- **Resolution & Fee Deductions**:
  - Returned packages are inspected at our warehouse before any resolution is applied:
    - **If customer requests reshipment**: Customer is responsible for the actual shipping fee incurred to resend the package.
    - **If customer requests order cancellation**: We refund the purchase amount **after deducting the round-trip shipping costs and applicable packaging/handling fees**.

### 4. Refund Method & Processing Time (Phương thức & Thời gian hoàn tiền)
- Once we receive the returned item and inspect its condition, we process the refund promptly.
- Refunds are issued back to the original payment method used (Credit Card, PayPal, Apple Pay, etc.).
- Processing window: typically 3–10 business days depending on the customer's financial institution.

### 5. Official Support Contact (Kênh liên hệ chính thức)
- For inquiries regarding order cancellation, returns, exchanges, or refunds, customers should contact our support team:
  - **Official Email**: `order@huykjeweler.com`
  - Support team assists with RMA verification and prepaid return labels for eligible cases.
