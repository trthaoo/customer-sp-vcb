# Warranty & Exchange Policy (US Market)
VIENCHIBAO – Official HuyK Jewelry Product Exchange & Warranty Policy

## Purpose
Official studio guidelines governing product exchanges, warranty coverage, repair craftsmanship, silver authenticity guarantees, and jewelry maintenance care.

## Policy Details
HuyK – VIENCHIBAO sincerely thanks our valued customers for supporting HuyK – VIENCHIBAO handcrafted jewelry. With our reputation, dedication, commitment, and extensive experience in the fine jewelry craft, we provide our customers with the following official Product Exchange and Warranty Policy:

### 1. Product Exchange Policy (Chính sách đổi hàng)
- **Exchange Window & Defect Terms**:
  - Customers may exchange a product **within 8 days** from the date of receipt if the product has an initial defect, such as deformation, scratches, damage, breakage, or manufacturing flaws.
  - Each product may be exchanged **only once**.
- **Exchange Value & Difference**:
  - Customers may exchange the item for a product of equal or higher value.
  - If the replacement item is of higher value, the customer is responsible for paying the price difference.
  - Any applicable shipping costs for customer preference exchange will be borne by the customer.
- **Exchange Conditions**:
  - The product must be returned in its original condition, without dents or deformation, and must remain usable.
  - Customers must provide the purchase invoice, original packaging, and any accompanying gifts (if applicable).
  - **NON-EXCHANGEABLE ITEMS**: Product exchanges **STRICTLY DO NOT APPLY** to:
    1. Products that have been custom engraved.
    2. Products modified or resized according to specific customer requests.
    3. Specially made-to-order items.
  - *Inspection*: VIENCHIBAO staff inspects the product to determine whether the defect was caused by the manufacturer workshop or by customer handling before accepting the exchange request.

### 2. Product Warranty Policy (Chính sách bảo hành sản phẩm)
- **Warranty Coverage & Defect Assessment**:
  - **Free 1-to-1 Replacement for Initial Flaws**: We provide a complimentary replacement if, upon receiving the package, the piece has an initial defect caused by our manufacturing craft (e.g., loose setting, casting flaw, clasp defect).
  - **Assessment Required**: When an issue arises (such as a loose or fallen stone), our craft team will inspect photos and purchase timeframe to determine the root cause:
    * If the defect is due to manufacturing craftsmanship within warranty: covered 100% by complimentary repair or replacement.
    * If damage resulted from customer wear accidents (severe impact, dropping, chemical exposure): complimentary warranty does not apply, but we can assist with repair at material cost.
  - **Repair Services**: VIENCHIBAO accepts warranty and repair requests for all authentic VIENCHIBAO products that have suffered wear or damage:
    * Soldering broken joints and chain links
    * Stone re-setting or missing stone replacement
    * Clasp repair and clasp replacement
    * Re-shaping, re-polishing, and surface refinishing
    * *Notice*: An applicable warranty or repair fee is determined based on the extent of damage and required materials.
- **Warranty Conditions**:
  - Proof of Purchase: Customers must prove that the product was purchased through our official system (order number or email).
  - Exclusions: If a piece has been severely damaged due to improper customer wear, severe impact, chemical exposure, or improper storage, VIENCHIBAO reserves the right to refuse warranty service.

### 3. Silver Authenticity & Studio Purity Guarantee (Cam kết chất lượng bạc S925)
- **Solid S925 Sterling Silver Guarantee**:
  - Ready-to-wear jewelry is handcrafted from authentic solid S925 Sterling Silver (92.5% pure silver), with a protective electroplated platinum/rhodium finish for tarnish resistance and radiant luster.
  - 100% hypoallergenic, nickel-free, and lead-free—completely safe for sensitive skin. Every genuine piece features the official studio "S925" hallmark stamp.
- **Custom Gold**:
  - Bespoke pieces can be crafted in solid 14K/18K gold with accredited third-party test certificates upon custom order.
- **10x Money-Back Guarantee (Cam kết đền bù gấp 10 lần cho bạc S925)**:
  - If any silver product purchased from Vien Chi Bao is verified by an accredited laboratory to be counterfeit or substandard silver purity, we unconditionally refund **10 times the full purchase value**. (Does not apply to custom gold or normal wear and tear).
- **Quick DIY Silver Authenticity Tests at Home (Mẹo phân biệt bạc thật tại nhà)**:
  1. *Hallmark Inspection*: Look for the sharp, laser-engraved "S925" hallmark on the inner band, clasp, or pendant bale.
  2. *Magnet Test*: Authentic sterling silver is diamagnetic and will **never attract a magnet**. If it sticks, it contains iron or nickel alloys.
  3. *Ice Thermal Test*: Silver has the highest thermal conductivity of any precious metal. An ice cube placed directly on silver melts noticeably faster than at room temperature.
  4. *Density & Acoustic Chime*: Real silver has a substantial hand feel and emits a clear, high-pitched ringing chime when lightly tapped, rather than a dull thud.
- **Promotions & Packaging**:
  - Every order includes an artisan presentation box. Complimentary 100% free insured shipping is provided for all US orders (we do not have a free gift policy).
