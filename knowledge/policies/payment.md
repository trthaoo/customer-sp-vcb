# Payment Policy (US Market)
VIENCHIBAO – Official HuyK Jewelry Payment & Handover Policy

## Purpose
Guidelines governing checkout payment methods, accepted digital payment gateways, and handover protocols for unsupported payment inquiries.

## Policy Details
- **Sole Supported Payment Gateway (Phương thức thanh toán được hỗ trợ duy nhất hiện tại)**:
  - **PayPal**: Currently, VIENCHIBAO accepts payments exclusively via **PayPal** (featuring full PayPal Buyer Protection for secure and reliable international transactions).
  - Customers can pay securely using their PayPal balance, linked bank account, or credit/debit cards processed through PayPal's secure gateway.

- **No Cash on Delivery (COD) (Không áp dụng thanh toán khi nhận hàng)**:
  - We **do not support Cash on Delivery (COD)** for domestic US or international orders due to carrier compliance, transit insurance regulations, and cross-border security standards. All orders must be prepaid prior to dispatch.

- **Unsupported Payment Methods & Mandatory Handover Protocol (Các trường hợp thanh toán khác - Fallback & Handover)**:
  - Direct non-PayPal payment methods—such as direct credit/debit card processing outside PayPal, direct bank wire transfer, Zelle, Venmo, or Buy-Now-Pay-Later installment plans (Klarna/Afterpay)—are **not currently supported** via automated checkout.
  - **HANDOVER RULE**: If a customer asks to pay via, inquires about, or insists on any payment method other than PayPal (e.g. asking for direct credit card, COD, bank transfer, installment, etc.):
    1. The studio concierge clarifies that our automated store currently accepts payment via PayPal (with full buyer protection).
    2. The system triggers a **Fallback & Handover** (`flag: handover`, `needs_human: true`, `status_badge: pending_handover`) so human support / studio artisan staff can directly assist and accommodate the customer's request.

- **Taxes, Currency & Zero Hidden Fees**:
  - All product prices are quoted in USD ($). We adhere to a strict policy of zero hidden fees or unexpected post-purchase charges.

- **Customer Support Contact**:
  - For special payment arrangements or assistance, customers are connected with a human concierge or may contact:
    - **Official Email**: `order@huykjeweler.com`
