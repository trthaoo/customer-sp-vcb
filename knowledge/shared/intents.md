# Intent Taxonomy (US Market)

## Purpose
Standard list of customer intent classifications recognized by the inbound social commerce pipeline.

## Defined Intents
- ask_price: Customer asks about product price, pricing tier, or purchase cost.
- ask_material: Customer asks about materials, silver purity, gemstone authenticity, or tarnish resistance.
- ask_size: Customer asks about ring sizing, dimensions, measuring finger circumference, or size availability.
- ask_stock: Customer asks about inventory availability, in-stock status, or backorder lead times.
- ask_shipping: Customer asks about shipping fees, delivery speed, carriers, or shipping destinations.
- ask_payment: Customer asks about payment gateways, credit cards, PayPal, Klarna installments, taxes, or COD availability.
- ask_order: Customer asks how to purchase, how to place an order, where to buy, or requests checkout link and guidance.
- ask_photo: Customer asks to see photos, real pictures, close-up details, or on-model shots of the jewelry piece.
- ask_silver_quality: Customer asks specifically about silver hallmark S925, DIY authenticity tests, or 10x money-back guarantee.
- ask_bulk_discount: Customer asks about savings when buying multiple pieces, combo sets, or corporate gifts.
- ask_legitimacy: Customer asks if the brand/store is legit, asks where the business is located, or seeks anti-scam reassurance.
- ask_return_warranty: Customer asks about return window, size exchange, repair service, or warranty policy.
- ask_store_location: Customer asks about the studio atelier address, workshop visit, or showroom location.
- general_compliment: Customer compliments the design, leaves warm feedback, or shares friendly social greetings.
- request_custom: Customer asks about bespoke crafting, custom sizing, engraving, or personalized requests.
- demand_human: Customer asks to speak with a human team member or probes about AI / bot automation.
- complaint_angry: Customer expresses dissatisfaction, reports damaged goods, requests an urgent refund, or alleges scam.
